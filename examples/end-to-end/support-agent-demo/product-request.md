# Product Request

Support agents spend too much time reading inbound tickets, deciding urgency, and writing the first response. We want a lightweight support triage assistant that helps them move faster without creating cases automatically when human approval is required.

## Business Outcome

Reduce time-to-first-response for inbound support tickets while keeping account-impacting actions under human control.

## User

Customer support specialist handling inbound tickets.

## Scope

- classify each ticket as `billing`, `technical`, or `general`
- assign priority as `low`, `normal`, or `urgent`
- draft a short first response grounded in the ticket content
- require approval before a support case is created

## Out Of Scope

- sending email to customers
- creating tickets in a real CRM
- persistent customer memory
- autonomous account changes
